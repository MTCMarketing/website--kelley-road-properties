About Kelley Road

Property management built around people.

Kelley Road manages apartments, townhomes and single-family homes across Massachusetts. We look after the day-to-day so that living in one of our properties is straightforward: maintenance handled properly, shared spaces kept well, and a team you can actually reach when something needs attention.

We work with residents and owners alike, and we try to make both sides of that relationship simple. Clear property information before you apply, a tenancy that runs without surprises, and honest answers when you have a question — that is the standard we hold ourselves to.

Every home we manage is looked after by people who know it. That is what keeps our communities in good shape and what makes the difference between a property that is administered and one that is genuinely cared for.

  • Responsive management

    Clear communication and practical support, from the first enquiry onwards.

  • Well-kept properties

    Attention to the homes themselves, the shared spaces and the maintenance behind them.

  • Straightforward renting

    Honest property information and an application process without the guesswork.

  • Local team

    Managed locally by people who know the neighbourhoods.
