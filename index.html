  • Applications are completed online. Find the property you are interested in, then follow the application link on its page. The exact steps and requirements are confirmed during the application itself.

  • Current availability is shown on each property page. If a listing does not show a status, or you would like the most recent position, contact our team and we will confirm it for you.

  • Viewing arrangements vary from property to property. Get in touch about the specific home you are interested in and we will let you know what can be arranged.

  • Pet policies are set per property rather than across the board. Please check the listing for the home you are considering, or contact our team to confirm before you apply.

  • Requirements are confirmed as part of the online application. If you would like to know what to prepare in advance, contact our team and we will talk you through it.

  • Residents can contact Kelley Road to report a maintenance issue. The process for your property is confirmed when you move in.
